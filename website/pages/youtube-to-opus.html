What is Opus?

Opus is a modern, open, royalty-free audio codec, standardised by the IETF in 2012 as RFC 6716. It's the audio behind YouTube, Discord, WhatsApp voice messages and most video calls, because it sounds excellent at low bitrates. At around 128 kbps most listeners can't tell Opus apart from the original recording. MP3 usually needs twice that to get close.

Why Opus is the best format for YouTube audio

Every other format is a conversion: YouTube's Opus audio is decoded and compressed again, and every lossy step throws a little information away. Opus from YouLoader is a copy. There's no decoding and no second compression, so there's nothing to lose.

How to save YouTube audio as Opus

  1. Download YouLoader and open it.
  2. Paste a link: a video, a playlist or a whole channel.
  3. Pick Opus. It's the default, with one quality setting: original.
  4. Press Enter. The .opus file lands in Downloads\YouLoader with tags and square cover art.

Will it play on my device?

Opus plays on Android, in every modern browser, in VLC and foobar2000, and in Windows 11's Media Player. If you use an iPhone, an older MP3 player or a car stereo, choose MP3 instead. You'll give up a little space, not audible quality.
