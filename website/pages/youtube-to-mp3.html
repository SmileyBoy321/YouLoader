How to convert YouTube to MP3

  1. Download YouLoader and open it. No installer, no account. The first launch fetches its helper tools once.
  2. Copy a YouTube link from your browser's address bar or the Share button. YouLoader notices a copied link and pastes it for you.
  3. Pick MP3 and a quality. 320 kbps is the default.
  4. Press Enter. The MP3 lands in Downloads\YouLoader, with the title, artist and cover art filled in.

Got a whole playlist? Paste the playlist link. Every song goes into its own numbered folder, and pasting the same link next month downloads only the songs added since.

Why a 320 kbps MP3 isn't “better than YouTube”

YouTube's best music stream is Opus at roughly 130–160 kbps. Converting it to MP3 can't add back detail that isn't there. The high bitrate only makes sure the conversion itself loses nothing you could hear. If your player supports it, saving as Opus gives you the original stream at less than half the size. The Opus vs MP3 guide explains the difference.

Why not just use an online converter?

Online converters are free because you are the product. To pay for servers and bandwidth they rely on aggressive ad networks: pop-unders, fake “Download” buttons, notification spam and redirects to dodgy sites. Many cap the length or quality, and they break whenever YouTube blocks their servers.

YouLoader runs on your own PC. That's why it has no server costs, needs no ads and has no queue, and why it can download whole playlists several songs at a time.
