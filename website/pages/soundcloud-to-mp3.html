How to download from SoundCloud

  1. Download YouLoader and open it.
  2. Copy a SoundCloud link: a single track, a set or album, an artist page, or their likes.
  3. Pick MP3 and a quality. 320 kbps keeps everything SoundCloud sends.
  4. Press Enter. Sets and artist pages go into their own folder, numbered in order.

Following an artist? Paste their page again whenever you like. YouLoader remembers what it already downloaded and fetches only new tracks.

About SoundCloud audio quality

SoundCloud streams most tracks at up to 128 kbps. That's the ceiling for every downloader, because nobody can get more than the site sends. YouLoader always takes the best stream available and saves it at a high MP3 bitrate, so the conversion doesn't lose anything you'd hear.

Tip: when an artist has switched on SoundCloud's own Download button, use that instead. It's often the original high-quality file, and it's the way the artist chose to share it.
