Privacy

No ads. No analytics. No tracking.

This isn't a privacy policy written to hide things. It's a plain list of everything that connects to anything, on this website and in the app.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics scripts, no ad networks, no tracking pixels.
  • No third-party requests. Fonts, images and styles are all served from this domain.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the download button links to the release file on GitHub. GitHub has its own privacy policy.
  • Like any web host, GitHub may keep short-lived technical logs, such as IP addresses, to keep the service secure. We can't see or use them.

The YouLoader app

  • No account, no telemetry, no crash reporting. YouLoader never sends anything about you or what you download to us.
  • When you download something, yt-dlp connects to that site (for example YouTube or SoundCloud) to fetch it.
  • It connects to GitHub to download its helper tools (yt-dlp, ffmpeg and Deno) the first time, to update yt-dlp once a day, and to check whether a newer version of YouLoader exists. These are plain downloads of public files.
  • Settings are saved on your PC in %AppData%\YouLoader\settings.json, and the helper tools in %LocalAppData%\YouLoader\tools. Delete those folders to remove every trace.

Questions
