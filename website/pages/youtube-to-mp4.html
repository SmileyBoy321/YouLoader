How to download a YouTube video as MP4

  1. Download YouLoader and open it. It's one portable file, nothing to install.
  2. Copy the video link and paste it into YouLoader. Add as many links as you like, one per line.
  3. Pick MP4, then a quality: Best available, 1080p, 720p or 480p.
  4. Press Enter. You'll see the video and audio download, then merge into one MP4 in Downloads\YouLoader.

Why many converters stop at 720p

For anything above 720p, YouTube sends video and audio as two separate streams. Online converters often skip the work of joining them and give you a low-resolution file that already has both. YouLoader downloads both streams and merges them with ffmpeg without re-encoding. It's fast, and the picture is exactly what YouTube serves.

Playing 4K AV1 files on Windows

YouTube's 4K and 8K streams mostly use the AV1 codec. Windows 11 plays AV1 out of the box. On Windows 10, install the free AV1 Video Extension from the Microsoft Store, or use VLC. If a file needs to play on anything, including older TVs and editing software, choose 1080p.
