How it works

Three steps. Zero detours.

No redirects through five other sites. Everything happens in one window on your own PC, and files land in your Downloads folder with cover art and tags.

  1. Paste a link

    A YouTube video, a playlist, a channel or a SoundCloud track. Paste several at once, one per line.

  2. Pick a format

    MP3 for music that plays on anything, from phones to car stereos. MP4 for video, up to 4K.

  3. Press Enter

    YouLoader downloads, converts and tags the file. Paste a playlist again later and it fetches only the new songs.

The rules

Free. For real.

Most “free” downloader sites make their money from the junk you click through. YouLoader has four rules instead, and they don't change.

  1. 01

    Free forever

    No trial, no premium tier, no feature held back to upsell you.

  2. 02

    No ads, ever

    No banners, no pop-unders, no fake download buttons, no browser notifications.

  3. 03

    No tracking

    No account, no analytics, no telemetry. The app only talks to the sites you download from and to GitHub for updates.

  4. 04

    Open source

    Every line is on GitHub under the MIT license. Read it, build it yourself, or make it better.

Why it's free

VLC has stayed free, ad-free and tracking-free for over twenty years. Its lead developer has reportedly turned down offers worth tens of millions to keep it that way.

YouLoader follows the same idea on a much smaller scale: a tool you use every day should simply work and respect you, without selling your attention to the highest bidder.

It also costs nothing to run. The code and this page live on GitHub, and every download happens on your own PC, not on a server someone has to pay for. So there's nothing to charge for, and nothing to sell.
